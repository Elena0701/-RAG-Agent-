SAMPLE SERVICES AGREEMENT

This fictional Sample Services Agreement is entered into on January 15, 2026,
between Sample Provider Ltd. (the "Provider") and Sample Recipient Ltd.
(the "Recipient"). This document is synthetic test data only.

1. Effective Date and Initial Term
This Agreement becomes effective on February 1, 2026. Its initial term expires
on December 31, 2027.

2. Renewal
After the initial term, this Agreement automatically renews for successive
periods of one year unless either party gives the other written notice of
nonrenewal at least 30 days before expiration of the then-current term.

3. Termination for Convenience
Either party may terminate this Agreement without cause upon 60 days' prior
written notice to the other party. This right is independent of the nonrenewal
notice in Section 2.

4. Services and Fees
The Provider will provide monthly support services. The Recipient will pay
a fixed monthly fee of USD 1,000 for those services.

5. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California.

6. Insurance
During the term, the Provider must maintain commercial general liability
insurance with coverage of at least USD 1,000,000 per occurrence for the
benefit of the Recipient.

7. Audit
The Recipient may audit the Provider's records relating to fees charged under
this Agreement once per calendar year upon 10 days' prior written notice.

8. Assignment
Neither party may assign this Agreement to a third party without the other
party's prior written consent.

9. No Intellectual Property License
The provision of support services does not grant either party a license to
the other party's intellectual property. No intellectual property license is
granted under this Agreement.

10. Confidential Information
Each party must keep the other party's confidential information confidential.
Upon termination, each party must return the other party's confidential
documents. This return obligation does not require transition assistance or
any continued services after termination.
